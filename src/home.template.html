About INPRECASA

Maritime safety is our business.

INPRECASA is the commercial brand of Industria de Prevención Canaria, S.L., a family-owned company based in the Canary Islands.

For more than three decades we have provided maritime safety and fire protection services to vessel operators, shipping companies and technical departments across the archipelago.

We operate from two bases — our headquarters in Tenerife and a second base in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria — combining technical expertise, inspection, maintenance, testing and safety equipment servicing under a single provider.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    How often do liferafts need servicing?

    As a general rule, SOLAS liferafts must be serviced at intervals not exceeding 12 months, and always according to the manufacturer's instructions and the vessel's flag requirements. We can review your equipment's due dates.

    Do you work on board or in your workshop?

    Both. Inspections can be carried out on board or in our workshops in Tenerife and Las Palmas, depending on the equipment and the scope of work.

    What information do you need for a quote?

    Vessel name and IMO number (if applicable), the service or equipment involved, the port and your preferred dates. Any previous service reports help us scope the work faster.
